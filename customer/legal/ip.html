Intellectual Property Policy

How Creatarsh handles client-owned materials, project deliverables, reusable components and third-party assets.

1. Client materials

Logos, photographs, text, trademarks, product information, databases and other materials supplied by a customer remain the customer's property or the property of its respective rights holder. The customer authorizes Creatarsh to use them only as reasonably necessary to perform the project.

2. Project deliverables

Ownership or licensing of final project deliverables is determined by the applicable quotation, invoice or signed agreement. Where a project document grants ownership after full payment, that grant applies only to the defined final deliverables and not automatically to third-party materials or Creatarsh's pre-existing tools.

3. Creatarsh pre-existing materials

Creatarsh may use reusable code, libraries, templates, frameworks, processes, know-how, components and internal tools. Unless expressly transferred in writing, these underlying materials remain with Creatarsh while the customer receives the rights necessary to use the delivered product.

4. Third-party and open-source materials

Open-source software, fonts, stock assets, APIs, plugins, SDKs, cloud services and other third-party components are subject to their own licenses and terms. Creatarsh cannot transfer rights that it does not own.

5. AI-generated materials

AI-assisted outputs may have uncertain ownership or third-party restrictions depending on the tool and jurisdiction. Where AI is used, the applicable AI Usage Policy and project agreement apply. Customers should review important outputs before commercial use.

6. Customer responsibility

The customer is responsible for ensuring that materials and instructions supplied to Creatarsh do not infringe another person's copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other rights.

7. Portfolio use

Unless a project agreement says otherwise, Creatarsh may identify completed work in its portfolio or marketing materials after launch. Customers requiring confidentiality or no-portfolio use should request it in writing before or during contracting.

8. Infringement concerns

If you believe content or a project asset infringes your rights, contact us with sufficient information to identify the material and explain the rights involved. We will review the concern and may restrict or remove disputed material while investigating.
