Questions, answered.

What to expect when planning and working on a Creatarsh project.

What services does Creatarsh provide?

We provide website and e-commerce development, custom business software, mobile applications, branding and graphic design, UI/UX, video editing, cinematography, digital marketing and ongoing technical support.

How do I start a project?

Contact us with a short description of your business and what you want to achieve. We will discuss requirements and recommend the next step.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, features, integrations, content, timeline and support requirements. Published packages apply only to the deliverables described in those packages. Custom projects receive a written quotation.

Do you take an advance payment?

For approved projects, the payment schedule and advance amount are stated in the quotation or agreement. Work begins after the agreed initial payment and scope approval.

How long does delivery take?

Timelines depend on the project scope and how quickly content, feedback and approvals are provided. The estimated timeline is confirmed in the proposal.

Can you build software specifically for my business?

Yes. We can discuss workflows such as CRM, ERP, inventory, billing, school management and other custom requirements. Features are confirmed after requirement discovery.

Will I own my website or software?

Ownership, source-code handover, third-party licences, hosting accounts and other project rights are defined in the signed service agreement or quotation. Ask us to clarify these before approval.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

We can help arrange hosting and provide maintenance under an agreed plan. Third-party hosting, domain, email, payment gateway and other provider charges may be separate.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Revision rounds and post-launch changes depend on the selected package or project agreement. New features or work outside the approved scope may require a separate quotation.

How can I contact Creatarsh?

Email creatarshbusiness@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +91 75667 43098. You can also use the project enquiry page.

Still have a question?

Tell us what you are planning and we will help you understand the next step.
